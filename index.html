Connections

We’ve personally done European connections around an hour before and made them without an issue. Airlines also generally account for minimum connection requirements when they sell an itinerary. That said, if you are not a frequent international traveler, we highly recommend leaning on the safer side and giving yourself more time between flights.

  • Try to stay at nonstop or one stop.
  • Compare total travel time.
  • Watch connection length.
  • Multiple stops introduce more opportunities for delays and baggage issues.

Apply for a U.S. passport (opens in a new tab)

If you already have one

Check the expiration date. We recommend at least six months of validity remaining after your May 2027 trip.

Renew a U.S. passport (opens in a new tab)

European entry rules generally require your passport to be valid for at least three months beyond your planned departure from Europe. Six months gives everyone a comfortable buffer. Eligible U.S. citizens can renew online for routine service, but online renewals can’t be expedited.

Processing times
  • Routine: 4–6 weeks
  • Expedited: 2–3 weeks (additional fee)
  • Mailing can add up to 2 weeks before processing and up to 2 weeks after the passport is issued

Can I book a flight before I get my passport?

Usually, yes. Most airlines don’t require a passport number when you buy your ticket.

  • The name on the reservation should match your passport.
  • Update your passport information with the airline once you receive it.
  • Double-check all traveler information before departure.

Why do we need a passport copy?

We’ll need a copy of your passport information page before the wedding so we can give the hotel the guest information it needs and make check-in easier. We’ll send separate instructions for securely submitting it.
